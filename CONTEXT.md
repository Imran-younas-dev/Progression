# Progression

Progression is a personal life plan: a person’s goals, the milestones that move those goals, and the next action for today.

## Language

### Life plan

**Life plan**:
The set of goals that belong to one person, shown in the order they choose.
_Avoid_: Journey record, twelve-goal path, multiple plans

**Goal**:
A meaningful outcome a person wants, with a reason and a picture of success.
_Avoid_: Challenge, objective, OKR

**Outcome**:
The observable result that means a goal has succeeded.
_Avoid_: KPI, activity count, score

**Category**:
A label on a goal, such as Career or Custom.
_Avoid_: Workspace, module

**My Journey**:
The screen that shows the life plan.
_Avoid_: Roadmap, dashboard

### Execution

**Milestone**:
A stage of a goal with a written sentence that says when the stage is done.
_Avoid_: Challenge, epic, phase

**Task**:
One concrete action inside a milestone.
_Avoid_: Occurrence, routine, habit, subtask

**Today**:
The screen of next actions from active goals.
_Avoid_: Inbox, daily planner

**Review**:
A short weekly note about what happened and what to focus on next.
_Avoid_: Retrospective, insights report

**Evidence**:
A note or link a person attaches to a task or milestone.
_Avoid_: File, attachment, proof upload

**Progress**:
Counts of completed milestones and tasks done in the current week.
_Avoid_: Percentage, streak, score

### Later

**Workspace**:
Tools that belong to one category, such as job applications for Career. Not part of the first release.
_Avoid_: Goal type, required layer
